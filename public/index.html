Tips to reduce food waste & speed up pickup

  • Post early: Share surplus 1 to 2 hours before the event ends to give volunteers time to mobilize.
  • Keep it warm: Hot foods must stay above 65°C in insulated containers for safe handover.
  • Clear pickup instructions: Mention gate numbers, landmarks, and contact numbers to avoid delays.
